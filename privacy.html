Privacy

Advantage AI is in a closed test. This page says what the app does with your information today.

What we keep

  • Your name, username and a scrambled copy of your password. Team players don't give an email.
  • An email address, only if you're a solo player and choose to add one.
  • The match videos you film or upload, and the reports made from them.
  • If you use the iPhone app and allow alerts, a code that lets us send alerts to your phone.

Who can see it

  • A report opens only for someone signed in as a player in that match, the person who filmed it, or their team's coach.
  • Match video needs a sign-in as one of those people too. Nothing is public except the example report.

Who helps run the app

  • Modal runs the app and analyses the video. Cloudflare R2 holds video on its way to Modal.
  • Match numbers, not video, go to Anthropic's AI, which writes the tips in your report.
  • Expo delivers alerts to iPhones.

Deleting your information

  • Delete any report, with its video, from the report itself.
  • Delete your whole account in the iPhone app, under Account. That removes your account and the reports you filmed. A match with a teammate in it stays for them.
