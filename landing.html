From the fence to your next practice

No sensors and no one filming for you. Just the phone you already carry.

  1. 1

    Film it

    Put your phone on the back fence, up high and centred, with the whole court in the picture. Record in the browser, or upload a video filmed that way. How to film

  2. 2

    Send it

    Upload the video and mark the court. The app finds the points, who won them and how each one ended.

  3. 3

    Fix it

    Each player gets their top fixes, ranked by the points they cost, with a tactic, a habit and a drill for each.
