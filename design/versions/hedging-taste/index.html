How to film your match

  1. Hang the phone on the fence behind one baseline, in the middle, about 8 ft up. A phone tripod that hooks onto the fence works well.
  2. Phone sideways, pointing at the far baseline.
  3. All 4 corners of the court and the net must be in the picture.
  4. Film in 4K at 30 fps.
  5. Press record, then don't touch the phone until the match ends.
